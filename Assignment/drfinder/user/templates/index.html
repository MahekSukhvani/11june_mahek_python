About DR.FINDER

Our Story

Founded in 2020, DR.FINDER was born from a simple yet powerful idea: healthcare should be accessible, transparent, and patient-centered. Our founders, a team of medical professionals and tech innovators, recognized the challenges patients face when trying to find the right healthcare provider.

Today, we're proud to serve over 50,000 patients and work with more than 500 qualified healthcare professionals across multiple specialties. Our platform has facilitated over 100,000 successful appointments, making quality healthcare more accessible than ever before.

🎯

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between patients and healthcare providers by offering a comprehensive platform that makes finding the right doctor simple, fast, and reliable. We believe everyone deserves access to quality healthcare.

👥

Our Team

A dedicated team of healthcare professionals, software engineers, and customer service specialists working together to revolutionize how patients connect with doctors. Our diverse expertise ensures the best user experience.

🌟

Our Vision

To create a world where quality healthcare is accessible to everyone, regardless of location or background, through innovative technology solutions that prioritize patient needs and outcomes.

🔒

Privacy & Security

We maintain the highest standards of data protection and patient privacy. All medical information is encrypted and stored securely, complying with HIPAA regulations and international privacy standards.

🏆

Quality Assurance

Every healthcare provider on our platform is thoroughly vetted and verified. We ensure all doctors are licensed, board-certified, and maintain excellent professional standards and patient satisfaction ratings.

🌍

Community Impact

We're committed to improving healthcare access in underserved communities through partnerships with local clinics, free health screenings, and educational programs about preventive care.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

You can book appointments directly through our platform by selecting your preferred doctor and available time slot. You'll receive instant confirmation.

Is telemedicine available?

Yes! Many of our doctors offer virtual consultations. Look for the telemedicine icon when booking your appointment.

What insurance do you accept?

We work with most major insurance providers. Check with your specific doctor's office for detailed insurance information.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule appointments up to 24 hours in advance through your patient portal or by calling our support line.

How secure is my medical information?

We use bank-level encryption and comply with all HIPAA regulations to ensure your medical information remains completely secure and private.
